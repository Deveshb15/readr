Reading

Reading is the process of taking in the sense or meaning of symbols. Long-haul flights are strange pockets of time where the internet disappears and attention returns, and we read the things we saved weeks ago.

Overview

COLLAPSED-SECTION-MARKER. For educators and researchers, reading is a multifaceted process involving word recognition, orthography, alphabetics, phonics, phonemic awareness, vocabulary, comprehension, fluency, and motivation. Reading at altitude is its own pleasure, with nothing competing for attention.

Other types of reading are not speech-based writing systems, such as music notation or pictograms; the common link is the interpretation of symbols to extract meaning from the visual notations.
